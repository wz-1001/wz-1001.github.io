About

Chemical engineering taught me to examine a complex process, identify where it loses efficiency, and design a solution to the problem. I transitioned into data science to apply the same approach to data.

My MSc dissertation examines the energy cost of machine learning. Where a random forest performs almost as well as a deep neural network, I ask how much additional carbon the larger model produces for that marginal gain. The aim is to allow practitioners to weigh this trade-off before committing to large-scale training.
